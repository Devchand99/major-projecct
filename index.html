Why Water Quality Matters

  • EC (Electrical Conductivity) indicates the amount of dissolved salts and is used to assess water purity.
  • TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) should generally be below 500 mg/L for safe drinking water, per WHO guidelines.
  • Water temperature affects dissolved oxygen levels and biological activity in natural waters.
  • High TDS or EC can indicate contamination from sewage, fertilizers, or industrial waste.
